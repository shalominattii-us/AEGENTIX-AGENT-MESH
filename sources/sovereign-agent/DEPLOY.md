# SOVEREIGN CLEAN CLAW — Deploy Guide
## Zero-installation. Cloud-only. No device contact.

### Prerequisites
- Kimi Allegretto or higher plan
- Phone or any browser
- 3 minutes

### Steps

#### 1. Go to kimi.com/bot
Open https://kimi.com/bot in your phone browser.

#### 2. Create New Bot
- Click **Create**
- Name: `Sovereign`
- Handle: `@sovereign_destiny_bot`
- Upload any image for avatar (optional)

#### 3. Configure System Prompt
Open `system_prompt.txt` from this ZIP.
Copy the entire contents.
Paste into the **System Prompt** field in Kimi bot settings.

#### 4. Import Memory
Open `memory.json` from this ZIP.
Copy the entire contents.
In Kimi bot settings, go to **Memory** → **Import** → paste the JSON.

#### 5. Save & Test
Click **Save**.
Send a test message: `/status`
Expected: "The Resolute Desk is active. I am Sovereign..."

### What He Can Do (No Local Install)
- Answer questions about Sovereign architecture
- Explain the 8 venues and their gate requirements
- Describe cabinet members and their portfolios
- Discuss TSL, ESC, Destiny, XRPL
- Help draft executive orders (text only)
- Guide you through deployments when you have hardware
- Remember your projects across conversations

### What He Cannot Do (By Design)
- Run code on your device
- Access your files
- Break your hardware
- Install anything locally
